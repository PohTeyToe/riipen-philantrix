Mandatory Reporting Requirements
Grant recipients must provide a detailed summary of their activities, including the extent to which they achieved their intended outcomes (as outlined in their Community Grant Program application).
The final report must demonstrate how the project addressed at least one of the following goals outlined in the Community Grant Program policy:
• Economic Prosperity and Innovation • Cultural Enhancement, Environment and Diversity • General Social Enrichment
The organization must provide a detailed budget summary, including invoices and receipts, with the final report; significant financial variances must be explained in full, along with supporting documentation if applicable.
