# Data sources

Everything here was collected or generated on 2026-10-15.

The organization, its people and its records are made up. No real person, participant or organization appears in the records. What's real is the vocabulary and data shapes (from Philantrix's public product screenshots), a few short quotes from philantrix.com, and a public list of common first names.

## Rerun

```
node data/derive-names.mjs <female.csv> <male.csv> --exclude <names>   # only if the name list changes
node data/generate.mjs              # fixed seed; writes the same bytes every time
node data/compute-reference.mjs     # writes figures.json and fails if a planted case is off
node data/draft-narrative.mjs       # calls the Claude CLI; redrafts until the check passes
node scripts/check-narrative.mjs    # the check CI runs
```

`derive-names.mjs` also leaves out first names of real people connected to the company. That list is passed on the command line and stays out of the repo.

## Choices the brief left open

- **Org name.** "Tall Pines Youth Mentorship" was the first choice. A real Tall Pines school serves children in Brampton, so the sample org is "Kestrel Lane Youth Mentorship (sample)".
- **Age breakdown.** Nothing in it is hidden. The age ranges are decided once from every period a reader can switch to (the quarter and its three months): while a range has 1 to 4 people in any of them, it's combined with the next range (the last with the one before), and every period shows the same ranges. A small group only ever shows as part of a bigger one, so it can't be worked out from the total or by comparing periods. `tests/privacy.test.ts` checks this by trying every dataset in a small range, the way a reader who knows the rule would. The rule used before (hide the next smallest cell) failed that check 3,309 times.
- **Narrative people counts.** A people-count slot of 0 renders "no", and 1 to 4 renders "fewer than 5".
- **Submissions per participant served.** Shown to one decimal, rounded half up (4.1).
- **Goal G1's value** is a count of people, so it follows the same "<5" rule in funder output. At 1 to 4, its status and forecast aren't shown either, since both are worked out from the count and could give it away (halfway through, a forecast of 6 means 3). A data note says why.
- **Inactive participant.** No record of any kind for 30 or more days before Sep 30 (last activity on or before Aug 31) and no exit date.
- **Exit date** is the last enrolled day. The participant who exits Jul 31 counts as retained in the July view.
- **Duplicates.** When one person is logged twice for the same session, the record counts once. If the copies disagree on hours, the one with fewer hours counts, so a mistake can't add hours. The rule reads only the records, never ids or save times, so the app, the reference and SQL pick the same copy.
- **Attended while not enrolled.** A record for someone not on the group's roster that day counts toward participants served and hours, but not toward the attendance rate, which counts only seats offered.
- **A goal on its due date.** The as-of date is the period's last day, so a goal due that day and not met is Past due, with no forecast.
- **Confidence improved.** Each participant's latest check-in in the period against their intake score. The same score, or a lower one, is not improved. Two check-ins on the same day: the lower score counts. Participants without an intake score are left out.
- **Assignment Submissions** come only from people who attended, on each group's hand-in day (Coding Club Thursdays, Mentor Circle Mondays, Weekend Leaders Saturdays).
- **Goal G4** stays under 79.5% in both states, so its rounded value never shows 80% while the goal is unmet.
- **Data notes.** They say how an answer changed the counting, so a finding nobody answered has none: the report counts only what was logged. A session answered "Ran, attendance not recorded" gets a note in periods that contain its date; only a template that reports sessions delivered is told it counted as delivered. Answers of "Left the program" get one shared note, with no group or date, in periods ending on or after the exit date. The G4 note shows wherever G4 is on the report (the foundation template), and so does a note for a people goal whose status is withheld. The duplicate gets no note; it changes nothing a funder reads.
- **Summary goals.** The summary gives each goal its target, due date and status, not its progress. Every goal starts Jul 1, so for the quarter its progress would repeat the period's own figure. Progress is in the goals table.

## Planted cases

All match the brief and are checked by `compute-reference.mjs` on every run.

- Sessions with no records: Coding Club Thu Jul 23, Mentor Circle Mon Sep 14, Weekend Leaders Sat Sep 19.
- Inactive, no exit date: P08 (last activity Thu Aug 20), P02 (last activity Tue Aug 25). Both Coding Club.
- Exits: P23 on Jul 31 (Mentor Circle), P37 on Sat Sep 12 (Weekend Leaders).
- Six August joiners: P05, P06, P12, P18, P20, P31.
- Duplicate: P03, Coding Club, Thu Jul 16 (records E0105 and E0106).
- Weekend Leaders has no sessions in August.
- Participants served aged 19-24: 3 in July, September and the quarter, 0 in August. So 16-18 and 19-24 show as 16-24 in every period: 15 for the quarter, 14 in July, 5 in August, 14 in September.
- Goals at Sep 30: G1 Past due (6 of 8), G2 On track (forecast 1,142 of 1,050), G3 Behind (forecast 303 of 360), G4 No due date (76%, or 79% with all findings answered).
- Check-ins in every period: 31 in July, 25 in August (Weekend Leaders has no August sessions), 34 in September, 37 participants across the quarter. P09, P13 and P17 attended every month and never filled one in.
